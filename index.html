The Beholden story

Serve from a place of honor.

“Utang ng loob” — a debt of gratitude — is at the heart of the name. In Byrne Joshua Al-ag’s business story, being beholden means serving with honor and giving back to the community.

Beholden Coffee brings its own Mount Apo beans together with curated imports, profiled by Eclipse Micro Roastery & Coffee Lab. At the coffee bar, guests can smell the grounds, choose their beans, and take part in an interactive pour-over ritual.

One place, three rhythms.

Coffee ritual in the morning, Filipino comfort through the day, and cocktails when the lights get warm.

Coffee Ritual

The dedicated pour-over bar, the Beholden Coffee Flight with a croissant or egg tart, and Davao Creamy Mocha — a modern take on traditional sikwate.

Filipino Kitchen

A broad Filipino mains menu with sisig, kare-kare, grilled seafood, and sinigang, alongside pizza and pasta for the whole table.

Cocktails After Dark

An evening cocktail program built around classics and seasonal signature cocktails, from coffeehouse day into bar-hour night.
