The Beholden story

Serve from a place of honor.

“Utang ng loob” — a debt of gratitude — is at the heart of the name. In Byrne Joshua Al-ag’s business story, being beholden means serving with honor and giving back to the community.

Beholden Coffee brings its own Mount Apo beans together with curated imports, profiled by Eclipse Micro Roastery & Coffee Lab. At the coffee bar, guests can smell the grounds, choose their beans, and take part in an interactive pour-over ritual.
